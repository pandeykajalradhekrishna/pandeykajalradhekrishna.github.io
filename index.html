01 · AI research agenda

I investigate how people, models, data, and enterprise constraints interact—and how AI product decisions can make that relationship more useful, trustworthy, and measurable.

Four questions that

Research ambition

My long-term goal is to build a rigorous body of applied research on how enterprise AI can strengthen human judgment without replacing context, agency, or accountability. I want to connect human–computer interaction, conversational intelligence, product analytics, and responsible machine learning into practical methods that product teams can use—not only to launch AI, but to determine when it is useful, how it should behave, and whether it deserves to scale.

Near-term inquiryEvaluation beyond model accuracy

Develop product-level measures that connect relevance, confidence, comprehension, task completion, and operational outcomes.

Applied contributionResearch methods for AI product teams

Create repeatable ways to combine interviews, behavioral signals, experiments, model evaluation, and system constraints in one decision framework.

Long-term directionHuman-centered enterprise intelligence

Study how recommendations and agentic systems can support complex work while remaining explainable, governable, and appropriately bounded.

Research principles

Research area · Human–AI interaction

Design machine intelligence around the realities of work.

I study where people hesitate, improvise, lose context, or compensate for a system—and where an ML-assisted recommendation can strengthen judgment without obscuring agency. The question is not simply whether a model can predict an action, but whether that action is relevant, explainable, and useful in context.

Research goalDefine when machine assistance improves judgment—and when it introduces new cognitive or operational burden.

Product contributionTranslate findings into interaction principles, evaluation criteria, and roadmap decisions for human-centered AI.

MethodsContextual interviews · Workflow observation · Friction mapping · Recommendation framing

EvidenceTask success · Confidence · Adoption · Time saved · Workarounds reduced
